The games

Six games for your people.

Five are live now, each on its own site. One is coming soon. Players need no app and never pay.

Tambola

Live now

The classic number game, also called Housie. Every player gets a ticket on their phone, and the software finds every winner.

  • Numbers 1 to 90 come from a public random source, so nobody can choose them.
  • Add your own video or message between numbers.
  • A free demo game for 10 people.
Open TambolaTry the demo

Bingo

Live now

Numbers are called out loud. Every player gets a card on their phone, and the software finds every winner.

  • Classic 75-number bingo on a 5 by 5 card.
  • You choose the winning patterns: Line, Four Corners, X, Frame or Blackout.
  • A free demo game for 10 people.
Open BingoTry the demo

Tic Tac Toe

Live now

A tournament for your whole company. Colleagues play head to head, every move has a clock, and the software names the winners.

  • A drawn match is replayed with the marks swapped, so every match has a winner.
Open Tic Tac Toe

Snakes and Ladders

Live now

Everyone gets their own board and plays at the same time. Each turn is a 20-second window, and the software finds every winner.

  • Every board has different snakes and ladders, and every board is equally hard.
Open Snakes and Ladders

Luck by Chance

Live now

A dice game of pure luck. Every round has a clock, a top-10 list updates live, and the software names the winners.

  • Equal scores are settled by clear rules, so the ranking has no ties.
Open Luck by Chance

Lucky Draw

Coming soon

A live draw that picks the winners in front of everyone.

You pay once for each game you run, before it starts. There is no yearly plan. Building a game is free. Prices are on each game's own page.
