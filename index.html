Compliance

Designed to follow India's DPDP Act, the EU GDPR and US privacy laws such as the CCPA.

This is what we do today.

  • Players read a short privacy notice and tick two boxes before they join: they have read it, and they are 18 or older.

Questions

Short answers.

What are the best online games for team building?

It depends on your group. Tambola and Bingo work when everyone plays together on one call. Tic Tac Toe is a one-against-one tournament. Snakes and Ladders lets everyone play at the same time. Luck by Chance is a quick dice game. All of them open in the phone browser, with no app to install.

How to run housie for 200 employees?

Housie is another name for Tambola. Choose the day and time, share one link with the people you invite, and everyone opens it on their phone. The software gives out the tickets and finds every winner. Tell us your head count when you plan, and we will help you set it up.

Is there an online tambola for corporate events in India?

Yes. Corporate Tambola is made for company events. Players join from a link on their phone, with no app, and they never pay. You pay once for each game you run, to IT Services by Rajat Agarwal, through Razorpay. A free demo for 10 people is available.

Can remote and hybrid teams play together?

Yes. Everyone opens the same link on their own phone, in the office or at home. You can run the game alongside your usual video call.

Do players need to install an app or pay?

No. Players open a link in their phone browser. They never pay or stake money. The organizer gives the prizes.

How much does it cost?

Building a game is free. You pay once for each game you run, before it starts. There is no yearly plan. The price is shown on each game's own page before you pay.

What happens to player details?

No personal details needed. All participant information is deleted within 24 hours of the game. The winners record, and the contact details a winner confirms for a prize, are kept for 365 days, then deleted automatically.
