Data deletion

Player details are erased within 24 hours after the game ends.

Names, any mobile numbers and invitation records.

  • Billing records are kept for audit and tax reasons.

Compliance

Designed to follow India's DPDP Act, the EU GDPR and US privacy laws such as the CCPA.

This is what we do today.

  • Players read a short privacy notice and tick two boxes before they join: they have read it, and they are 18 or older.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • No advertising trackers. We never sell data. We never e-mail players.
  • A player can leave, ask what we hold, or ask us to delete it. We answer within 7 days.
  • A named Grievance Officer answers privacy complaints.
  • Players never pay or stake money. The organizer gives the prizes. We provide software only.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. Choose a game

    Pick one, then set the day, the time and the size.

  2. Invite your people

    Share one link with your people. You decide who joins.

  3. Play together

    Players join on their phones. The software finds the winners.
