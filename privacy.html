Data deletion and privacy

What we keep, and for how long.

No personal details needed. All participant information is deleted within 24 hours of the game. Here is the whole list, in plain words.

WhatHow longThen
WhatPlayer details: names or nicknames, any mobile numbers, phone codes, invitation records and requestsHow longWithin 24 hours after the game endsThenErased
WhatA game that never starts: its player detailsHow longWithin 24 hours after it was meant to endThenErased
WhatThe winners record: who won which prize, by numberHow long365 daysThenDeleted automatically
WhatContact details a winner confirms to receive a prize (Tambola, Bingo, Snakes and Ladders)How long365 daysThenDeleted automatically
WhatOrganizer details and game settingsHow longUntil 365 days after the game, or until the organizer closes the accountThenDeleted
WhatBilling recordsHow longKeptThenFor audit and tax reasons

Tic Tac Toe and Luck by Chance keep final results as numbers, without names.

Smaller records

  • A protected record of what happened in each game, kept to keep play fair: 365 days. Its personal part goes with the player details.
  • Visits to public pages: 365 days, then only anonymous totals.
  • Speed and error reports: 90 days. The mail log: 3 days.

In Tambola the organizer can also delete all player data at any time from the console.

What we do not do

  • We never e-mail players.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We run no advertising trackers.
  • We do not show players' mobile numbers to other players.
  • We do not use players' details to train an AI.

A player's rights

A player can leave at any time, ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We answer within 7 days. Players must be 18 or older, and tick a box to say so before they join.
