Data deletion and privacy

What we keep, and for how long.

Everything about your players, including the IDs you invited, is erased within 24 hours after the game ends. Here is the whole list, in plain words.

WhatHow longThen
WhatPlayer details: the IDs you invited, any names or nicknames, any mobile numbers, phone codes, invitation records and requestsHow longWithin 24 hours after the game endsThenErased
WhatA game that never starts: its player detailsHow longWithin 24 hours after it was meant to endThenErased
WhatThe winners record: who won which prize, by numberHow long365 daysThenDeleted automatically
WhatOrganizer details and game settingsHow longUntil 365 days after the game, or until the organizer closes the accountThenDeleted
WhatBilling dataHow longKeptThenWe keep billing data for audit and tax reasons.

Tic Tac Toe and Luck by Chance keep final results as numbers, without names.

Smaller records on the game websites

  • A protected record of what happened in each game, kept to keep play fair: 365 days. Its personal part goes with the player details.
  • Visits to public pages of the game websites: kept 365 days by the host, then only anonymous totals. This home site does no visit counting of its own.
  • Speed and error reports: 90 days. The mail log: 3 days.

In Tambola the organizer can also delete all player data at any time from the console.

Our promises to you

  • We never e-mail players.
  • Your data is never sold.
  • Our pages carry no advertising trackers.
  • This website takes no payment and has no sign-in. You sign in and pay inside your console. Payments are collected by IT Services by Rajat Agarwal, through Razorpay.
  • A player's mobile number is never shown to other players.
  • Players' details are never used to train an AI.

A player's rights

A player can leave at any time, ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We answer within 7 days. Players must be 18 or older.

This website: cookies and your device

This website uses no advertising cookies, no trackers and no visit counting, and its own code sends no information anywhere. Like any website, our host keeps ordinary server records of page requests. The Sign in link is a plain link you choose to press: it tells the console only that you came from this site, and, if you said Yes, that you have visited before. Your device keeps nothing until you press a button or answer the cookie question.

  • Always, when you ask for it: your light or dark choice and your Retro or Modern choice, when you press those buttons.
  • Your answer to the cookie question, so you are not asked on every page.
  • Only if you say “Yes, remember me”: a small note that you have visited, so the Sign in button can take you straight to your console next time. It is removed when you change your answer to No.
  • Only if you say Yes: an offline copy of this site’s own pages, so they open again without internet. Saying No removes it.

You can change your answer at any time with “Cookie choices” at the bottom of every page. These notes live on your device only, so they stay yours.
