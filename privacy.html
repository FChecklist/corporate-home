Privacy and Data

Players can join with just an employee ID or an e-mail. No personal details needed. All participant information is deleted within 24 hours of the game.

How a game uses data

  • The organizer sets up the game and decides how participants join: with an employee ID, with an e-mail, or open to anyone.
  • In a game open to anyone, the participant's details are used to identify the participant, and the organizer sees who joined.
  • All participant data is auto-deleted within 24 hours of the game.

After the game

The organizer and the Hosts receive the game report as a PDF, within 3 hours of the end of the game. If the e-mail bounces, the report is held and sent again after 24, 36 and 72 hours. Co-hosts do not receive it.

The report has the statistics, the game log, how the game was played and the winners in full. It has no row for any individual participant.

Tic Tac Toe and Luck by Chance keep final results as numbers only.

Smaller records

  • An audit log of what happened in each game (without any personal details), kept to keep play fair: 365 days.
  • All personal data of participants (if any) is auto-deleted within 24 hours.
  • Speed and error reports: 90 days.
  • The mail log: 3 days.
  • Navigation data is collected to improve the games.

In all the games the organizer can also delete all player data at any time from the console, once the game is over or if it was never published.

Good to know

  • Players are never e-mailed.
  • Personal data is never sold.
  • No advertising trackers are used.
  • Players' data is not shown to other players.

Who is responsible

The organizer who sets up a game decides why and how players' details are used in that game. The software is provided as a service under the brand name "IT Services by Rajat Agarwal".

A participant's rights

A participant can leave at any time, ask what is held, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Requests are answered within 7 days.
