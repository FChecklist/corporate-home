How it works

Three steps.

You set it up. Your people join with one link. The software runs the game.

  1. Choose a game

    Pick one of the games. Set the day, the time and the size. Building a game is free.

  2. Invite your people

    Share one link by WhatsApp, e-mail or your intranet, with the people you invite. You decide who joins.

  3. Play together

    Players open the link on their own phone. No app to install. A clear screen shows what is happening, and the software finds every winner.

After the game

You get a report by e-mail a few minutes after the game ends.

No personal details needed. All participant information is deleted within 24 hours of the game. See what is kept.

Paying

You pay once for each game, before it starts. There is no yearly plan. Read how paying works.

Players never pay. The organizer gives the prizes. We provide the software only.

Prices are on each game's own page.
